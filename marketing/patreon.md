## About (O stránce)
Mud & Iron is a free pixel-art WW1 trench war played on Instagram.

Comment HEGEMONY or CONCORD under a post on @mudandiron and you get your own soldier on a living front at mudandiron.web.app. Every morning at 13:00 UTC the Trench Gazette prints what happened in the night: who was hit, who held the line, which side took the next hill. Every soldier keeps a diary that grows day by day, and when a soldier falls, the next one in the family takes up the rifle.

It is made by one person, every day. No ads, no pay-to-win, nothing to buy in the game.

Patreon keeps the front running: the servers, the art, the videos and the time it takes to write a new edition every morning.

**What you get**
🎗️ The War Bonds ribbon for your soldier: next to the name on the front, on the soldier card and on the "I ENLISTED" share image.
📰 A thank-you in the next Trench Gazette.
📓 A line in your soldier's diary that stays there for good.

It is honour only. A ribbon never makes a soldier shoot straighter or live longer. Everyone on the front plays the same game.

## Jak se udělí stužka (beze změny proti Ko-fi)
Patron napíše na Patreonu své IG jméno → uživatel ho pošle Claudovi → příkaz do `data/commands.json`:
`{ "id": "support-<handle>-<datum>", "cmd": "support", "handle": "<ig>" }` (smyčka do ~5 min; stužka, zápis v deníku, poděkování v novinách).

## Až bude stránka hotová
1. Ověřit adresu stránky a případně ji opravit v `theme.support.url` a v `marketing/make_support.py` (`URL`).
2. Zapnout tlačítka na webu: `theme.support.buttons: true` („☕ Support“ u lupy a „Support the front on Patreon“ v panelu náboru).
3. Odkaz v biu IG.
4. Post `support-patreon` jen se souhlasem a časem.
